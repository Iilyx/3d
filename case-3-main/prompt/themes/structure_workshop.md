# Structure — Workshop (Studio)

Hint only. Use `timeline` when Source has ordered timeboxes. Do not invent timings. An unordered list stays `card_row`.

1. `title` — outcome + duration/seats/outputs in takeaway
2. `section` — why we’re here
3. `scorecard` — three outcomes, each with a `hint` sentence
4. `timeline` — 4–6 steps, `kicker` is the timebox from Source, `body` is one sentence
5. `card_row` — exercise A
6. `card_row` — exercise B
7. `callout` — mid-gate
8. `table_hero` — owners / follow-ups
9. `closing` — owned outputs + 3 close beats

Facilitation rhythm — not dark signal charts.
